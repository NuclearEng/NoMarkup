=== PRIVACY ===
This summary is stored in the app for when https://no-markup.com/privacy cannot be loaded. It lists the same categories as the in-app privacy manifest.

The app collects:
- Name, email, phone, and a user identifier for your account
- Date of birth for the 18+ check
- Government-ID or license images you submit for verification
- Precise job-site check-in location, and coarse location for the market picker
- Physical address details needed for a job or local pickup
- Photos and videos you choose for a job, listing, profile, chat, claim, or verification document. On a device that can screen images, an upload that cannot be screened or that is flagged as sensitive is refused. Flagged images stay hidden until you choose to show them
- Chat and other content you post
- Payment metadata and purchase history processed by Stripe. The app does not store your full card number
- A device identifier so push notifications can reach this phone
- Customer-support messages you send us

We use this information to run the marketplace, take payments and escrow, verify eligibility, send transactional notices, and handle reports and disputes. Marketing is optional.

Service providers that process personal data for us, including Stripe, hosting, and error monitoring, must protect it to the same or equal standard described in the privacy policy. We do not sell personal information.

Account data is kept while your account is open. Signed-in users can export data or schedule deletion under Account. You can turn optional activity recording on or off under Account, Request log. That log stays on this device.

For privacy requests, email support@no-markup.com.
