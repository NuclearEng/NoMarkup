## What they are actually buying / selling

| Rail | What a UHNW party expects |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Goods** | Authenticity, escrow until handoff, privacy of location, no flea-market noise |
| **Services** | Vetted providers, reverse auction that still feels controlled (not chaos), contracts that stick |

## Design north star

**Quiet power.** Hermès window · Swiss private desk · Apple industrial design.  
Not casino gold, not crypto neon, not “luxury” as a filter on Amazon.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| One mark, perfect materials | Busy seals, tiny microcopy on icons |
| Gold as *metal*, navy as *ink* | Muddy bronze, gray chrome |
| Space, type hierarchy, calm | Scaffold debug panels as Home |
| Escrow language as trust | “Web handoff” apologetic CTAs |
| Photography that could hang in a gallery | Stock clutter, low-res grids |

**Home screen icon:** must still **register in a sea of apps**. Current master is **champagne brushed gold + crystal M↓** — metal chroma + monogram silhouette. Diamond-as-centerpiece is **not** the icon; reverse-auction down-arrow is the product signal.

---

## Product bars (if a billionaire listed tomorrow)

1. **Escrow is non-negotiable** — funds held until pickup confirmation; refunds/disputes are institutional, not forum drama.  
2. **Identity & reputation** — trust tier, history, verified seller — visible without doxxing.  
3. **Privacy** — approximate location for search; exact address only to counterparties who need it.  
4. **Condition & provenance** — photos, notes, serials; counterfeit/stolen report path is real.  
5. **White-glove path** — high-value listings should feel “private client,” not “garage sale with Stripe.”  
6. **Services reverse auction** — price goes *down*, but award is *chosen* (trust + bid), never pure race-to-bottom trash.
