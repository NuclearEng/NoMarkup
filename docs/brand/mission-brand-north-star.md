# NoMarkup brand north star (mission-true)

## The product

| Who | Outcome |
|-----|---------|
| **Customers** | Market competition **drives costs down** (reverse auction) |
| **Independent contractors & small businesses** | Compete on merit → **better wages / take-home** than lead-gen markup platforms |
| **Platform** | **NoMarkup** — no extractive middleman tax on either side |

**Not:** luxury jewelry · private billionaire vault · flea-market chaos.  
**Is:** fair price discovery for real-world work and local goods.

---

## What the diamond got wrong

A solitaire on champagne silk reads **Cartier / high jewelry**.  
That’s prestige, not “my plumber gets paid fairly” or “I stopped overpaying for HVAC.”

UHNW *can* use the platform; the **brand must still make sense for a solo electrician**.

---

## Brand pillars

1. **No middleman** — name is the promise.  
2. **Competition with dignity** — reverse auction lowers *customer* price; providers still win on quality/trust, not only race-to-bottom.  
3. **Two-sided fairness** — save on services / earn without lead-gen skim.  
4. **Trust & escrow** — settlement is serious; look is clean, not casino.  
5. **Local & real** — jobs and pickup, not abstract crypto luxury.

---

## Visual direction (corrected)

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Clear **N** or **NM** monogram | Diamond-only (generic jeweler) |
| **Downward** energy (price) without looking “discount bin” | Gavel clichés, neon sale tags |
| Navy + gold as *craft / confidence* | Silk plaque jewelry vibe as *only* story |
| Simple silhouette at 60pt | Over-ornate seals that mush |
| Working-pro polish (Stripe / Square / Thumbtack-tier clarity) | Hermès window as sole metaphor |

**Gold** = quality and trust, not “only the rich.”  
**Navy** = seriousness and stability.

---

## Copy voice

- “Providers compete. You save.”  
- “Fair work. Fair price.”  
- “No lead-gen markup.”  
- Not: “Private market for serious value” as the *only* line (too exclusive).

---

## Icon brief (next masters)

Must answer in one glance:

> **NoMarkup — reverse auction — fair market for services.**

Candidate forms:

1. Bold **N** on navy or gold field + subtle **downward chevron** (price down).  
2. **N** with dual arrows (cost ↓ / wages ↑) if still legible at 60pt.  
3. Clean **NM** monogram, no gem.  
4. Optional small **gold mark** of quality — not a centerpiece diamond.

Diamond may return as a *tiny* quality accent later; it must not *be* the brand.
