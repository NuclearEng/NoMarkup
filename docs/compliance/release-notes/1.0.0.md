## ASC "What's New" — paste block (v1.0.0)

```text
Welcome to NoMarkup 1.0 — a local marketplace for jobs and goods. You pay the price you agree to. The platform fee comes out of the seller payout.

• Post a job and let local pros compete: reverse-auction bidding drives your
  price down, not up.
• Shop the goods marketplace: bid live on local items or Buy Now, with pickup
  near you.
• Pay with confidence: escrow-protected checkout via Stripe, with Apple Pay
  support.
• Chat directly with providers and buyers — offers, photos, and scheduling in
  one thread.
• Verification Center: providers build trust with license, insurance, and ID
  verification badges.
• Track auctions from your Home and Lock Screen with widgets, and follow live
  bidding with Live Activities.
• Move faster with App Shortcuts: check in to a job, open your bids, or jump to
  your watchlist from Siri and Spotlight.
• Sign in with Apple, Face ID protection for sensitive actions, and
  notifications for outbid alerts, awards, and messages.
```

*(8 bullets + greeting — fits ASC's 4000-char limit with large margin. Trim the
last bullet first if a shorter variant is needed.)*

## App Store description — paste block (not entered in App Store Connect)

```text
NoMarkup is a local marketplace for jobs and goods. Post a job and let nearby
pros compete on price, or bid on items you can pick up nearby. You pay the
price you agree to. The platform fee comes out of the seller payout.

Pay for real-world jobs and goods with Apple Pay. Chat in the app, report or
block someone who breaks the rules, and follow auctions from the Home Screen
with widgets, Live Activities, and Control Center controls. Sign in with Apple.
There is no in-app purchase in this version.
```

This text is a draft for the founder. It is not a claim that the App Store
Connect record, screenshots, or privacy label have been entered.

## Scope notes (internal — do not paste)

| Bullet | Product surface it reflects |
|--------|-----------------------------|
| Reverse-auction jobs | `/jobs` post → descending bids → award → contract |
| Goods marketplace | `/marketplace` forward-auction + Buy Now, 25 mi local pickup |
| Escrow payments | Stripe PaymentSheet + Apple Pay (`RailACheckout`); funds held in escrow until pickup/completion confirmations |
| Chat | In-app channels/messages with report + block |
| Verification Center | License / insurance / government-ID document verification (`VerificationCenterView`) |
| Widgets + Live Activities | Active Bids + Next Closing widgets (Home/Lock Screen), auction Live Activity with Dynamic Island |
| App Shortcuts | Check In to Job, Open My Bids, Open Watchlist, Post a Job intents |
| Sign in / security | Sign in with Apple, biometric gate for sensitive actions, push notifications |

**Do not mention** (not in binary / hard-off): BNPL, working capital, insurance
purchase, legal services, lead generation, instant payout, StoreKit
subscriptions (free-tier-only build), passkeys (server not ready — button
dead-ends).
